Hey,

I’m Siza

— a data engineer & AI/ML researcher exploring the intersections of AI Safety and Ethics, Healthcare Informatics, and Human–Computer Interaction.

I believe that the future of intelligent systems lies not only in their capabilities, but in their alignment with human values — systems that are safe, transparent, and truly assistive. I like my code explainable, my coffee dark and hot, and my designs intentionally imperfect.

Research Interests

  • AI Safety & Trustworthiness: Designing systems that remain safe and reliable in high-stakes domains like healthcare, ensuring factual correctness aligns with ethical integrity.
  • Healthcare Informatics: Leveraging machine learning for clinical decision support while prioritizing privacy, fairness, and patient-centric transparency.
  • Human–Computer Interaction (HCI): Studying how people interact with intelligent systems, and creating adaptive interfaces that respond meaningfully to human context and emotion.
  • Responsible AI: Exploring frameworks that embed explainability, accountability, and social responsibility within large-scale AI systems.
