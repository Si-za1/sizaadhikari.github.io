Research Interests

With my work centred around healthcare projects, and prior research in healthcare communication and adaptive systems, I am curious to explore this intersection further. My research interests include — but are not limited to — the following areas:

Health Informatics / AI in Healthcare: I focus on practical applications of machine learning in clinical environments — from Clinical NLP and large language models in medicine to AI-driven clinical decision support and patient-facing tools. My work aims to make Healthcare AI that is useful, evidence-driven, and safe for clinicians and patients alike.

Human-Centered AI / Human-in-the-loop: Designing AI systems with humans at the center — creating human-in-the-loop workflows, running usability studies, and ensuring AI-assisted clinical decision support prototypes actually help end-users. I combine HCI principles with applied ML to improve trust and adoption.

AI Regulation, HIPAA & Privacy: Researching privacy-preserving machine learning, HIPAA-compliant model deployment patterns, and governance frameworks for healthcare AI. I’m interested in approaches that balance utility and compliance so systems can be both effective and legally/ethically sound.

EHR & Clinical Data: Working with Electronic Health Records and messy clinical datasets to build predictive models and workflow tools that support hospital operations and patient care. This includes feature engineering for EMRs, predictive modeling, and designing analytics that respect clinical realities and data governance.

Keywords (examples): Healthcare AI, Clinical NLP, Large Language Models in Medicine, Human-centered AI, Human-in-the-loop, HIPAA-compliant AI, Privacy-preserving ML, EHR analytics.
